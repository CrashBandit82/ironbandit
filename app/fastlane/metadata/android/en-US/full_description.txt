IronBandit is a free, ad-free workout log for strength training. It works offline and without an account: all data stays on your device, the app has no internet permission.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Workout plans like "Push Day" – only exercises and their order; start a plan and work through it exercise by exercise, with workout duration</li>
<li>Log weight and reps set by set, prefilled from your last set and your last workout</li>
<li>Rest timer with a notification and vibration when the rest is over, also in the background</li>
<li>Weekly overview with a weekly goal, volume compared to last week and a hint when an exercise stalls</li>
<li>Lasting notes per exercise, 22 starter exercises and as many of your own as you like</li>
<li>Body weight with history and 7-day trend</li>
<li>Import and export plans as CSV</li>
<li>Backup and restore as a file, CSV export</li>
<li>German and English, light and dark theme</li>
</ul>
